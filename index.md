## About Me

I am an undergraduate student in computer science with a growing interest in large language model systems and AI infrastructure. I care about turning research ideas into efficient, reliable, and reproducible systems.

My current learning and research practice spans model post-training, inference systems, agent architectures, and the systems techniques that connect them.

## Research Interests

- **LLM Systems & AI Infrastructure:** training and inference efficiency, memory management, model serving, and distributed systems.
- **Agent Systems:** agent architectures, tool use, scheduling, context management, and Agent OS.
- **Efficient Learning:** supervised fine-tuning, knowledge distillation, LoRA/QLoRA, and mixture-of-experts routing.
- **Research Engineering:** reproducible experiments, clear evaluation, and open technical artifacts.
